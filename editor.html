1 · What it does

One sentence. What does it do for the person who has to run this thing?

Tracks whose turn it is on the chore wheel and sends exactly one nudge — not a guilt trip.
2 · Who it's for

Who is this for? Just you, your household, or a group you're part of?

Me and my two kids at home
3 · Must do

3–7 things it has to nail, no excuses. One per line.

When I mark a chore done, it should move to the next person automatically. When it's Sunday, it should show the week's schedule. When someone's chore is overdue, it should show it in red.
4 · Must not do

What it should not do. Two things is enough.

It should not send texts outside our household. It should not need anyone to create an account.
5 · Screens

Name the main screens. Sketches are drawn later.

This week's schedule. Add a chore. Who's turn is it. Settings.
6 · Done when

How we'll know it's actually working — not just installed. A few plain checks.

I can add a new chore and assign it to a person. The app shows the right person's turn. My kid can mark their chore done from their phone.
