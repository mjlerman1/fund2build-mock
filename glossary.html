What badges mean

High-level only. This page does not describe how screens work internally.

Safety cleared — concept and prototype code passed the public bar. Not a guarantee the software is “safe” beyond that status.

In review — not listed for pledges yet.

Blocked — fail closed. Not in discovery. No pledges, no factory.

Goal met · cap stays — pledges reached unlock; factory token cap does not rise if you overpledge. Surplus is held until the creator claims it or funds future updates.
