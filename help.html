Help

How it works

People back a written idea. Money pays for sketches, then a prototype, then a build with a fixed budget. When it's ready, everyone can use the app and the code is public.

Stage words

  • Idea — written up and checked. Backing pays for sketches next.
  • Sketches — screens are drawn. Backing pays for a working prototype next.
  • Prototype — you can click through it. Backing goes toward the build.
  • Funded — goal reached and the maker approved. The build starts automatically.
  • Building — being built and tested to a fixed budget.
  • Ready — built, checked, and yours to use.
  • Paused — backing is paused while we review.
  • Closed — this one stopped. A reason line says why.

How money works

You get
A web app you can use and share. Free, open source, yours to keep.
By
Built to a fixed budget once the goal is reached. Typical build time: a few days.
If not
Money for steps that never happen comes back. Steps that were made stay public.

We check every idea for safety before it's listed and again before it's released. If our build breaks, we fix it at our cost. Extra money never buys a bigger build.

Sort

Hot is recent attention. New is newest listings. Top is lifetime upvotes (toggle $ when Top is on). Ranking does not unlock a build.

How hosted access works

The maker is paid month by month while the site is online. Offline 14 days, or never online → unused months refunded.
