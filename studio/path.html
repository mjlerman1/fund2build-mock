What are backers paying to create?

Two listing types so we can feel the UX. Pick one and walk the wizard + a public pledge page.

Open source

Public good

Pledges pay the factory. After ship, anyone can use, fork, or self-host. No perks. If it needs an API, users bring their own keys.

Hosted / paid

Crowdfund a service

You set backer tiers (a year free, a discount). Non-backers may pay later. Source might stay private. Ongoing host/inference is part of the listing.

Then compare pledging: PantryLoop (OSS) vs SkyDigest (hosted).
